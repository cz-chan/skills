# Product

Recetario lets home cooks save recipes, find them later, and share them through a link.

## Problem

Home cooks lose recipes across screenshots and chats.

## Solution

Cooks save their recipes in one place and find them there later. To share one, they send a link.

## Why this product

Compared with a notes app, the person reading a shared recipe does not need an account.

## Out of scope

- Menu planning.
