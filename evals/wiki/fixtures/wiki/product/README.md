# Product

Recetario lets home cooks save recipes and share them through a link.

## Problem

Home cooks lose recipes across screenshots and chats.

## Solution

One place to keep recipes, and a link to share each one.

## Why this product

Sharing works without the reader creating an account.

## Out of scope

- Meal planning: nobody asked for it in the first version.
